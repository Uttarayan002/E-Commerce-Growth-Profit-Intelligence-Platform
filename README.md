# E-Commerce-Growth-Profit-Intelligence-Platform  (ON GOING)
> Transforming transactional data into strategic business decisions through analytics, visualization, and predictive modeling.

> This project is designed to reflect the responsibilities of a modern Data Analyst while incorporating selected Data Science techniques where they provide measurable business value.
The focus is not only on analyzing historical data but also on generating actionable insights that support strategic decision-making across executive, marketing, operations, and product teams.

# Project Overview
Businesses often experience increasing sales while struggling to improve profitability. Revenue alone doesn't explain business health-customer behavior, product performance, operational efficiency, and marketing effectiveness all contribute to sustainable growth.
This project simulates the role of a Data Analyst working within an e-commerce company. The objective is to investigate key business challenges, identify growth opportunities, and deliver actionable recommendations supported by data.
Rather than focusing solely on dashboards or machine learning models, this project follows a complete analytics workflow from understanding the business problem to presenting executive-level insights and predictive solutions.

# Business Problem
The executive leadership team has identified several concerning trends:

- Revenue is growing, but profit margins remain inconsistent.
- Customer acquisition is increasing while repeat purchases are declining.
- Return rates have increased across several product categories.
- Marketing spend continues to rise without proportional business growth.
- Inventory decisions are based on historical assumptions instead of data-driven insights.

The business requires an analytical solution to answer:
What is driving business performance, where are the hidden inefficiencies, and what actions should leadership prioritize to improve profitability and customer retention?

# Business Objectives

This project aims to:

- Analyze sales, customers, products, and operational performance.
- Design meaningful business KPIs for executive reporting.
- Discover revenue and profitability drivers.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Evaluate marketing effectiveness.
- Identify operational bottlenecks.
- Recommend strategic actions supported by data.
- Develop predictive models that assist future decision-making.

# Business Data Model
The project uses a relational data model that simulates an e-commerce business. 
The dataset is organized into multiple interconnected tables representing customers, 
orders, products, marketing, payments, suppliers, and returns. This structure enables 
end-to-end business analysis across sales, customer behavior, profitability, operations, 
and marketing performance.

### Core Tables 
| Table       | Description                                             |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Customers   | Stores demographic and customer profile information.    |
| Orders      | Stores order-level transaction details.                 |
| Order Items | Stores individual products purchased within each order. |
| Products    | Contains product catalog and pricing information.       |
| Returns     | Stores returned products and refund details.            |
| Marketing   | Stores customer acquisition channels and campaigns.     |
| Payments    | Stores payment method and transaction status.           |
| Suppliers   | Stores supplier information for inventory analysis.     |
